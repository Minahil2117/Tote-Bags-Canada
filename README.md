# Tote Bags Canada — B2B Wholesale & Custom Manufacturing Website

Official B2B single-page website repository for **Tote Bags Canada** (Project ID: `PRJ-001 / CCT-01`), developed for client **Mr. Wasi** and managed under **SYNNC. Product & Engineering**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
A focused, high-converting B2B wholesale platform dedicated to one core product family: **Tote Bags**.
* **Target Audience:** Businesses worldwide, corporate procurement, retail brands, creative agencies, and event organizers.
* **Core Offering:** Wholesale bulk supply, custom printing, embroidery, and bespoke manufacturing.
* **Primary Conversion:** Compact Wholesale RFQ (Request for Quotation) engine routed directly to sales.
* **Official Domain:** `totebagscanada.com`
* **Direct Sales Desk:** `sales@totebagscanada.com` | `+1 (236) 513-9254`

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Prominent Brand Identity:** High-resolution official logo (`images/logo.png`) and favicon (`images/favicon.png`) with clean, visible branding.
* **Global B2B Headline & Messaging:**
  > *"Custom & Wholesale Tote Bags for Businesses Worldwide"*
  Positioned for international commercial clients without unconfirmed shipping guarantees.
* **Curated Design & Customization Examples (One Core Family: Tote Bags):**
  1. *Heavy Contrast Boat Tote* — Classic structured tote with reinforced contrast trim and heavy canvas.
  2. *Custom Sized Canvas Lineup* — Tailored dimensions from compact event giveaways to oversized utility carriers.
  3. *Blank Wholesale Stock* — Clean unprinted blanks in standard sizes, ready for fast dispatch and local printing.
  4. *Artisan Embroidered Edition* — Precision multi-thread embroidery, monograms, and custom zip closures.
  5. *Everyday Promo Cotton* — Cost-effective natural, white, and black cotton flats for trade shows and brand activations.
  6. *Appliqué & Custom Patches* — Woven and embroidered patches with reinforced hardware for retail brands.
  7. *The Eco Jute Artisan Tote* — Textured natural jute/burlap with reinforced carrying handles.
  8. *Premium Retail Canvas Tote* — Fashion-forward construction tailored for boutiques and merchandise programs.
* **Two Streamlined Customer Pathways:**
  * **Route 1: Bring Your Own Design** (Bespoke custom manufacturing with vector artwork upload)
  * **Route 2: Explore Our Design Examples** (Order blanks or adapt shown styles)
* **Compact & Functional RFQ Form:**
  * Clean, mobile-optimized form with essential fields only:
    * Full Name
    * Business Email
    * Company Name
    * Country (International dropdown)
    * State / Province / Region
    * Phone Number (Optional)
    * Order Quantity (Quick chips: `25`, `100`, `500`, `1,000`, `5,000`, `10,000+` and custom numeric input)
    * Customization / Enquiry Details
    * Artwork Upload (Optional: PDF, AI, EPS, SVG, PNG, JPG up to 25MB)
  * **Live Routing:** Submits enquiries asynchronously to `sales@totebagscanada.com` via FormSubmit with full loading indicators, success state, and fallback error handling.
* **Accurate Content Standard:**
  * No unverified claims regarding specific fabric weights, dimensions, turnaround hours, or unconfirmed MOQs.
