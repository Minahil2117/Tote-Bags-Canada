How Custom Branding Works

We apply your brand logo, typography, or artwork directly onto tote bags using confirmed production techniques.

01

Precision Screen Printing

Ideal for clean spot color graphics, corporate logos, and bold promotional designs. Produces sharp, durable prints suitable for volume merchandising.

02

Direct Embroidery & Monograms

High-density thread stitching applied directly onto heavy canvas or stitched patches. Provides a tactile, premium finish for corporate gifts and boutique retail.

03

Full-Color Digital Transfer

Recommended for complex illustrations, gradients, and photographic artwork where multiple colors and detailed nuances need to be faithfully captured.

We support flexible quantities ranging from smaller initial batches (25, 100, 500 units) to large-scale commercial runs (1,000, 5,000, and 10,000+ units) with tiered volume pricing.

Do you accept international orders?

Yes. While our brand is Tote Bags Canada, we serve businesses internationally wherever fulfillment and delivery logistics are confirmed with the client.
