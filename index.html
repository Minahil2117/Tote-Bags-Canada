Bespoke Branding Capabilities

How Custom Branding Works

We turn your logo or original artwork into finished wholesale tote bags using verified printing and embroidery methods.

01

Precision Screen Printing

Ideal for clean 1 to 6-color spot graphics, corporate logos, and bold promotional messaging. Durable inks that withstand repeated laundering without cracking or peeling.

Best For: High-volume runs, trade shows, retail packaging.
02

Direct 3D Embroidery & Monograms

High-density thread stitching directly onto heavy canvas or stitched patches. Delivers a tactile, executive, and timeless aesthetic for premium merchandise.

Best For: Heavy boat totes, boutique retail, executive client gifts.
03

Full-Color Digital Transfer

Perfect for photographic artwork, gradients, and intricate multi-color illustrations. High-resolution reproduction capturing every color nuance accurately.

Best For: Illustrated merchandise, artist collabs, detailed event graphics.
